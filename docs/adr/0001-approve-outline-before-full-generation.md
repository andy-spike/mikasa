# Approve the Outline before full Course generation

Mikasa creates the Course specification and Outline first, then waits for the Learner to approve the Outline. It generates Lessons one at a time in reading order (ADR 0009 supersedes the original parallel execution), reviews the complete Course, makes targeted corrections, and publishes one Course revision only after the checks pass. Learning dependencies determine the reading order, and with sequential generation they are also the execution order. We rejected on-demand Lesson generation because it cannot review or correct the Course as a cohesive unit.
